Privacy Policy

Robotics Terms Simple does not collect or transmit personal data to the developer. The app is designed to work without an account, backend, advertising, or analytics.

On-device

Study data

Progress and settings remain on your device.

By choice

Speech access

Microphone and speech recognition permissions are requested only for spoken-answer practice.

None

Tracking

No account, backend, ads, analytics, or developer tracking.

What the app includes and stores

The app includes 64 robotics terms bundled with the download. It does not fetch this study content from a remote service.

Your study progress and app settings are stored locally on your device. Robotics Terms Simple has no user account and no developer-operated backend.

Optional spoken-answer practice

Spoken-answer practice is optional. The app requests microphone and speech recognition permission only when you choose to use that practice feature.

The app requests on-device speech recognition. If on-device recognition cannot be used, the app treats speech recognition as unavailable rather than choosing cloud recognition. You can continue studying and reveal the definition without speech recognition.

Audio and transcripts are not stored by the app, sent to the developer, sold, or used for advertising or analytics.

Apple device services

Microphone access and speech recognition are Apple system services controlled by your device permissions. Although the app requests on-device recognition, Apple may process device-level services under Apple’s own terms and privacy policies. The developer does not receive audio or transcripts from those services.

You can review or change microphone and speech recognition access at any time in the Settings app on your device.

Collection, sharing, and advertising

The developer does not collect or transmit personal information through Robotics Terms Simple. The app has:

  • no account or sign-in;
  • no developer-operated backend;
  • no advertising or advertising identifiers;
  • no analytics or tracking; and
  • no sale of personal information.
