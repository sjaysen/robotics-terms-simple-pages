VerseCraft Privacy Policy

Last updated 20 August 2026

VerseCraft does not collect any information about you. Nothing at all.

What we collect

Nothing. VerseCraft has no account, no sign-in, and no way to identify you. We do not ask for your name, your email address, your location, or anything else.

What leaves your phone

Nothing. VerseCraft does not connect to the internet. Every question and every verse is stored inside the app itself, which is why it works in Airplane Mode. There is no server to send anything to.

Your scores

Your accuracy, your best run, and your progress in each subject are saved on your own phone so the app can show them back to you. They are never uploaded anywhere. Tapping "Clear my progress" inside the app deletes them, and deleting the app removes them too.

Advertising and tracking

There is none. VerseCraft contains no advertising, no analytics, no crash reporting, and no third-party software development kits of any kind. Nobody is watching what you do in this app, including us.

Children

VerseCraft is safe for all ages. Because it collects no information at all, it collects none from children either.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will appear at this address with a new date at the top.
