A Bible quiz. Three answers, one right.

Getting in touch

Email jay@bettercontext.ai and you'll get a reply. Telling us your iPhone model and the app version (shown on the About screen) helps.

Common questions

Do I need an internet connection?
No. Every question and every verse is inside the app. It works in Airplane Mode.

Do I need an account?
No. There is no sign-in and nothing to register.

Which Bible does it use?
The World English Bible, a modern revision of the American Standard Version of 1901. It is in the public domain.

Where are my scores kept?
On your phone only. Nothing is uploaded. "Clear my progress" on the progress screen erases them, and deleting the app removes them too.

I think an answer is wrong.
Please email us the question and we will check it against the text. Every question carries the verse it came from, so mistakes are easy to track down.

Privacy

VerseCraft collects nothing at all. See the privacy policy.
