Help with the app

Support

Find quick answers for studying, sound, and spoken-answer practice. If you still need help, send an email and describe what happened.

Quick help

Robotics Terms Simple keeps its controls direct. These are the most useful things to check when a feature does not behave as expected.

Spoken-answer practice is unavailable

Open the Settings app on your device and check that Robotics Terms Simple has permission to use the microphone and speech recognition. Spoken practice requires Apple’s on-device speech recognition to be available. If it is not available, you can keep studying and use Reveal instead.

Definitions are not read aloud

Check the mute control inside the app and your device volume. Text-to-speech playback does not require microphone permission.

A term needs another pass

Choose Practice again to return the term to your study flow. Choose Got it when you are confident with the definition.

Progress and settings

Progress and settings are stored on your device. There is no account or online backup to sign in to.

Contact support

Include your device model, iOS version, and the steps that led to the problem. Do not include passwords or other sensitive information.
